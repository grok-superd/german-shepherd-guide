About Shepherd Guide

Shepherd Guide is a small, independent website about German Shepherd Dogs, written for owners and would-be owners in the UK, the US and elsewhere.

Why this site exists

There is a lot of information about German Shepherds online, and much of it is repetitive, unsourced or written mainly to sell something. This site aims to do the opposite: give clear, practical guidance and link every important fact to a source you can check, such as the kennel clubs that publish the breed standards, veterinary organisations and peer-reviewed research.

How we write and check content

  • Sources first. Facts and figures come from organisations such as The Royal Kennel Club, the American Kennel Club, the FCI breed standard, the Royal Veterinary College's VetCompass research, PDSA, VCA Animal Hospitals and the American Veterinary Society of Animal Behavior. Each page lists its sources at the bottom.
  • No invented numbers or quotes. If we cannot find a reliable source for a claim, we either leave it out or say clearly that it is a rule of thumb or opinion.
  • Where sources disagree, we say so. For example, our lifespan section explains why kennel club and veterinary research figures differ.
  • Regular review. Each page shows the date it was last reviewed. If you spot an error or an out-of-date link, please let us know.

Who runs this site

Shepherd Guide is run by Ryan Longman, an independent developer based in the UK. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any kennel club, breed society, veterinary body or pet food company.

Not veterinary advice

Nothing on this site replaces advice from a qualified vet who has examined your dog. Please read our terms and disclaimer.
