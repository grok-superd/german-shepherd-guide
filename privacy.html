Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit Shepherd Guide, including through cookies and third-party advertising, and the choices you have.

Effective date: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Shepherd Guide ("we", "us") is an independent information website run by Ryan Longman in the United Kingdom. The site address is https://grok-superd.github.io/german-shepherd-guide/. For privacy questions, see our contact page.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and the site has no forms that collect personal information. If you email us, we will receive your email address and anything you include in your message, and we will use it only to reply.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address for security and to operate the service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. The site itself does not set cookies. However, if advertising is enabled, third parties may set cookies and use similar technologies as described below.

Advertising, including Google AdSense

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. If we do:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info (US) or www.youronlinechoices.com (UK and EU).
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law, including for visitors in the UK, the European Economic Area and Switzerland, we will ask for your consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, through a consent message from a certified consent management platform. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time through that message.

Analytics

We do not currently use analytics tools. If we add them in future, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent.

Legal basis (UK GDPR)

Where we process personal data, we do so on the basis of your consent (for non-essential cookies and personalised ads) or our legitimate interests in operating and securing the site and replying to messages.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise them, contact us. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). US visitors may have additional rights under their state's privacy laws.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

External links

Our pages link to other websites, such as kennel clubs and veterinary organisations. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
